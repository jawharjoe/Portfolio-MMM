Working approach

How I approach an engagement

01 · Frame the decision

Start with what will change.

Agree on the business question, decision owner, target metric, constraints and what evidence would be sufficiently persuasive.

02 · Understand the data

Test availability and assumptions.

Review data structure, unit definitions, confounders, time dynamics, measurement gaps and the limits of identification.

03 · Model and validate

Compare credible approaches.

Establish a clear baseline, evaluate uncertainty and generalization, and validate against independent evidence where available.

04 · Deliver and enable

Make the analysis usable.

Translate the model into scenarios, dashboards, documentation, handoffs and clear recommendations—including reasons not to act when evidence is weak.
