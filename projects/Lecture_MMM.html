MMM Foundations with Adstock and Saturation

Estimate channel impact on sales or conversions and translate model outputs into budget guidance.

Open notebook view Download ipynb
Technologies: matplotlib, numpy, pandas, scipy, sklearn

Pros

  • Interpretable coefficients and clear contribution story
  • Works well as a first MMM baseline
  • Easy to communicate to non technical stakeholders

Cons

  • Sensitive to multicollinearity and missing variables
  • Needs careful handling of lag and diminishing returns
  • Assumes historical relationships hold in future

Stakeholder insights and comments

  • Identify top incremental channels by contribution and marginal return
  • Detect saturation where extra spend gives smaller lift
  • Use holdout validation to avoid overclaiming impact

Suggested stakeholder framing: what changed, why it matters, and what decision to take next.

Advice to management

  • Reallocate budget from saturated channels to higher marginal return channels
  • Run scenario planning for spend shifts across channels
  • Align measurement window with business cycle and promos

Improvements and next steps

  • Add adstock and saturation transforms if not present
  • Add seasonality, pricing, promo, and macro controls
  • Use Bayesian MMM for uncertainty bands and stronger priors
