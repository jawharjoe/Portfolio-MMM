Technologies: matplotlib, pandas, seaborn, sklearn, statsmodels, xgboost

Pros

  • Improves planning for spend, inventory, and staffing
  • Highlights seasonality, trends, and shocks
  • Provides confidence intervals when supported

Cons

  • Performance degrades with structural breaks and policy changes
  • External drivers may be missing
  • Overfitting risk with many features

Stakeholder insights and comments

  • Use rolling backtests to pick models not a single split
  • Track error by horizon to set realistic expectations
  • Combine forecasts with business rules for extremes

Suggested stakeholder framing: what changed, why it matters, and what decision to take next.

Advice to management

  • Use forecast to set guardrails for spend and targets
  • Refresh models on a fixed cadence and monitor drift
  • Create an alert when actuals diverge from forecast bands

Improvements and next steps

  • Add holiday, promo, and exogenous regressors
  • Ensemble Prophet and SARIMA with simple averaging
  • Automate retraining, backtesting, and reporting
