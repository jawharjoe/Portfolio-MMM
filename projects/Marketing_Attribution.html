Attribution Modeling

Marketing Attribution with Markov Chains and Heuristics

Explain how channels assist conversion paths and quantify their share of credit across touchpoints.

Open notebook view Download ipynb
Technologies: matplotlib, numpy, pandas, seaborn

Pros

  • Captures upper funnel assists beyond last click
  • Works with path data and is easy to update
  • Provides channel level removal effect style intuition

Cons

  • Path data quality and identity stitching can bias results
  • Does not automatically give incrementality without experiments
  • Cross device tracking gaps can distort paths

Stakeholder insights and comments

  • Compare first touch, last touch, and Markov style credit to see assist patterns
  • Spot channels that start journeys versus close journeys
  • Use results to refine campaign objectives and creative strategy

Suggested stakeholder framing: what changed, why it matters, and what decision to take next.

Advice to management

  • Use attribution to guide targeting and sequencing, not as the only budget allocator
  • Pair with geo tests or lift tests to validate incrementality
  • Report both volume and efficiency metrics for decisions

Improvements and next steps

  • Add time decay and path recency weighting
  • Segment by cohort, region, or product line
  • Validate with experiments and calibrate channel priors
